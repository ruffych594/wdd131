1. Site Name

Business Data Insights

The name describes the website's focus: helping small businesses gain useful insights from their data. It is clear, professional, and easy for the target audience to understand.

BDI

Logo concept: The letters BDI stand for Business Data Insights. Navy blue represents trust and professionalism, while teal represents growth and progress.

Proposed domain: businessdatainsights.example (illustrative only; no domain has been purchased or checked).

2. Site Purpose

Business Data Insights will introduce small business owners and beginners to practical data analysis. It will explain how to understand customers, analyse sales and products, monitor business performance, and make informed decisions.

The website will include beginner-friendly explanations of key performance indicators (KPIs), examples of charts and reports, and simple ways to apply data to everyday business decisions. JavaScript features will include an interactive profit margin calculator and a short quiz on business metrics. The website will be responsive so it works on mobile phones and desktop computers.

3. Scenarios

These are questions that visitors from the target audience may ask:

  • How can I calculate my business's profit margin?
  • Which products generate the most revenue for my business?
  • Which key performance indicators should I track each month?
  • How can charts help me understand changes in my sales?

4. Color Schema

The design uses navy blue as the main brand color, supported by teal and a light background for readability.

Navy blue — #123047

Used for the header, navigation, main headings, footer, and important text on light backgrounds.

Teal — #20B2AA

Used for buttons, highlights, links, and chart accents.

Light background — #F4F7FA

Used for the page background and content areas to keep the layout clean and readable.

5. Typography

Poppins — headings

Used for the site title, section headings, and smaller card headings. Its bold, modern appearance gives the site a clear visual hierarchy.

Open Sans — body text

Used for paragraphs, lists, and explanatory content because it is easy to read on mobile and desktop screens.
